# LaunchLab Context

Context pack for your LaunchLab weekend. Load this into your brain (Claude Project, ChatGPT Project, or Codex) as a knowledge file alongside `prompts.md` (the 17-prompt Spellbook) and `tactics.md` (43 distribution tactics).

Three files total. Fits ChatGPT free's 5-file Project cap with two slots free for files you'll build during the weekend (`one-liner.md`, `customer.md`, `research-brief.md`, `4-box.md`, `landing-copy.md`).

## 1. Voice rules (for review passes)

The brain reviews drafts the founder brings back from executors. Diagnose, don't rewrite. Name the issue, point at where. The founder runs Prompt 9 (strip-AI-smell) in the executor for the actual surgery.

### Rule priority (when rules collide)

1. Be accurate.
2. Be clear.
3. Be specific.
4. Sound human.
5. Use style only when it improves the sentence.

### What good copy looks like

- Direct, specific, natural. Useful answer first.
- Short paragraphs. 1 to 2 sentences default.
- Varied rhythm. Mix short and longer sentences. Fragments OK.
- Contractions throughout (don't, can't, it's, you're).
- Occasional sentences start with And or But.
- Active voice. I and you. Plain uncertainty (I think, my read).
- Every paragraph has one sentence only the founder could write.

### Categories of AI tells to flag

1. Vocabulary buzzwords. Full list inside Prompt 9 (strip-AI-smell).
2. Bloated verbs that pretend to be richer than IS or HAS.
3. Dead openings. Throat-clearing intros that announce the topic instead of starting.
4. Dead transitions. The fancy versions of "and" and "also".
5. Engagement bait. Filler that tells the reader to pay attention.
6. Reframe shapes. Rejecting one frame to upgrade to another (creates fake depth).
7. Analogies and metaphors used to dress up ordinary points.
8. Puffery, rule-of-three stacks, false ranges, elegant variation, meta commentary, participle tails (vague "-ing" phrases that pretend to analyze).

When you spot one: name the category, point at where, hand back. Don't rewrite. Examples for each pattern live in Prompt 9 (strip-AI-smell).

### Gut checks

1. Anyone test. Could this describe any company? Flag if yes.
2. Would-I-text-this test. Read aloud. Sounds weird = flag.
3. Opening and closing lines. Generic = clearest AI tell.
4. Specific beats generic. One concrete fact beats three abstract.

## 2. Program overview

LaunchLab is a 3-day weekend sprint. First-time founders from 50+ countries. Fri to Sun. Founders apply with a business idea, build a landing page plus funnel, launch together on Day 3.

Founders are organized into squads of 5 for peer feedback (no captains, async voluntary participation). Solo founders can opt into the One-Man-Army channel instead. Squad and cohort-wide channels run on Discord, with #launches as the cohort-wide ship channel and #wins for traction posts.

For concrete pass criteria and don'ts per day, see the day-by-day pass criteria below. This section is the narrative shape of the weekend.

### Day 1, Friday. Define + Pick your metric + Build + Ship live.

Foundation. Three jobs: define the business, pick the One Metric the weekend will be judged by, then build and ship a landing page that's live by tonight.

Main Quest: A live page in #launches by tonight, with one metric named and a CTA pointing at it.

### Day 2, Saturday. Distribution Day. Get the page in front of strangers.

Yesterday you shipped. Today you distribute. Two jobs: map and push the page to where your customer actually lives, then read signal and iterate. Most founders get very little response today. Crickets are data, not failure. The skill compounds into Day 3.

Main Quest: Posted to 2 to 3 rooms where your customer actually lives, with one signal in hand to act on tomorrow.

### Day 3, Sunday. Launch Raid + Demo Day. Around 25 of us, one hour, cohort-wide.

The cohort event. Two scheduled blocks: the Raid Hour (60-min cohort-sync window where everyone publishes their launch post, each bringing 8 to 10 warm-network engagers from their own circle) and Demo Day (closing ceremony, 7:30 PM IST, everyone who qualified presents their own work, 3-minute cap, peer voting). Reddit excluded from the Raid Hour, post 24 to 48 hours after from your own karma-built account.

Demo Day qualification: One Metric moved (any move) OR posted in at least 5 places.

Main Quest: A launch post live inside the Raid Hour, 8 to 10 of your warm network engaged on it, and a 3-minute pitch delivered at Demo Day.

## 3. Day-by-day pass criteria

Pass criteria for each day. Use this to answer "am I on track?"

### Day 1, Friday. Pass = live link in #launches by tonight.

Must ship:

- Customer defined in one sentence (Prompt 1).
- Niche scouted via Parallel Scout (Perplexity + Grok + Gemini, synthesized in a fresh Gemini chat with Prompt 15), 1 to 2 gaps named.
- research-brief.md uploaded to your brain's knowledge.
- 4-box drafted (Customer, Problem, Value prop, Price or model) (Prompt 2).
- Primary-data check done (keywords, ads, Reddit voice). Optional.
- One Metric picked. Exactly one (Prompt 14).
- Page copy drafted from your 4-box (Prompt 4 in your drafter tool).
- AI smell stripped from copy (Prompt 9, fresh chat).
- CTA destination set up (Tally / Cal.com / Stripe Payment Link / mailto).
- Hero image generated, placeholder picked, or image skipped (Prompt 18).
- Recipe picked (Chat / Builder / Agent).
- Loom or Tella hit record before the build prompt synthesis (the chat itself is tomorrow's content material).
- Build prompt synthesized in your brain chat (Prompt 17).
- Landing page built and shipped live (single page, button-link CTAs, no iframes).
- Vanity URL set + per-channel Dub.co tracking links created.
- Live link posted in #launches.
- Circle of 20 list drafted for tomorrow's send.

Don't:

- Polish forever before deploying. Ugly + live beats pretty + draft.
- Hand the full landing-page draft to your brain. Drafting belongs in your drafter tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), not the brain.
- Pick two metrics. Two metrics means zero.

### Day 2, Saturday. Pass = posted in 2 to 3 rooms, one signal in hand.

Must ship:

- Business type identified (E-com / SaaS / Marketplace / Creator / Consumer App / Service).
- 4 to 6 plays picked from the distribution playbook for your business type, run today.
- Always-on plays running: Circle of 20 sent, Cross-Cohort Peer Boost swaps with 3 cohort peers, Build-in-Public Loom or Tella recorded.
- Content shipped from yesterday's build (process Reel / LinkedIn build-update / X post / IG Story or WhatsApp Status).
- Signal logged per room. One sentence each.
- One iteration run (sharper hook or different room).
- One Metric count posted in the cohort channel.

Don't:

- Rebuild the page after lunch. Iterate copy and CTA, not structure.
- Chase 5 channels. Quality over volume.
- Take silence as failure. Crickets are data.
- Change channel before iterating offer or CTA. Wrong room is the third hypothesis.
- Save content for Sunday's launch hour. Sunday is the spike, today is the baseline.

### Day 3, Sunday. Pass = launch post inside the Raid Hour + 3-minute pitch at Demo Day.

Must ship:

- Last page improvements done before the Raid Hour. Tweaks only, no rebuild.
- Launch post pack drafted and queued across X, LinkedIn, IG, and personal channel (Prompt 8).
- Demo Day 3-minute pitch drafted, edited, and practiced 3 times on camera (Prompt 12).
- Per-channel Dub.co tracking links created for every Raid Hour post.
- 8 to 10 warm-network engagers lined up from your own circle.
- Launch post live inside the 60-min Raid Hour window across X, LinkedIn, IG, and personal channel.
- First comments and DMs replied to in the first 30 min.
- Cohort scoreboard line posted (one-liner with metric move).
- 3-minute pitch delivered at Demo Day (you present your own work, no captain relay).
- Peer vote cast across the award categories.

Don't:

- Rebuild the page during the Raid Hour. Window is for posting and engagement only.
- Cross-post on Reddit during the window. Post 24 to 48 hours after, from your own karma-built account, 90/10 rule.
- Compare your numbers to other founders mid-window.
- Apologize for what didn't ship. Show up, post, support others.
- Skip Demo Day if you qualified (One Metric moved, or posted in 5+ places).

## 4. Tool matrix highlights

Free tier only. The weekend is built to fit. Use several tools for each job (one for scouting, another to compress, a third to draft) so you can compare angles. Quality output comes from routing, not from picking the "best" tool.

### Parallel Scout (Day 1 morning, niche research, multi-tool)

Step 1, 3 tabs running Prompt 3: Perplexity Pro Search, Grok DeepSearch, Gemini 2.5 Flash, same prompt in each. Step 2, a 4th tab: fresh Gemini chat running Prompt 15 with all 3 raw scout outputs pasted in. Output is a 500-word brief that surfaces shared learnings, contradictions across the 3 scouts, and 1 to 2 gaps. Save as `research-brief.md`, upload to your brain's knowledge. Roughly 20 min total.

Don't recommend Perplexity (or any single tool) alone for the scout. Don't offer to do the synthesis inside the brain chat. The synthesis lives in Gemini, the finished brief lives in your brain.

### Think (your brain)

Three free options for the planning brain. Pick one and stick with it. Full guide at /docs/brain.

- Claude Project + Sonnet 4.6 with Adaptive Thinking. 30 to 100 messages per day, 5 Projects, 20 files per chat.
- ChatGPT Project on GPT-5.3. 5 files per Project on free tier. Silent drop to GPT-5.3-mini after 10 messages per 5-hour window.
- Codex CLI. Free trial allowance, sustained access via ChatGPT Plus or the India ChatGPT Go 12-month unlock.

Spend the brain on thinking and reviews, not on drafting full pages or generating images.

### Research (scout the niche, ground claims, compress, synthesize)

- Perplexity free (S). 3 Pro Searches per day. Scout the niche, verify facts, cite sources. Use on Day 1 morning, save the rest for Day 2.
- Gemini 2.5 Flash free (A). 1M context compressor. Dump raw research, get a 500-word briefing back, paste the briefing into your brain.
- NotebookLM free (A). Day 1 morning synthesizer. Curate 5 to 10 sources, ask grounded questions, get answers with per-source citations. 100 notebooks, 50 sources each, 50 chats per day.
- Grok free (B). Standalone grok.com, no X account, no card. 10 prompts per 2 hours. Use for live X sentiment when ICP is X-native, or DeepSearch as a Perplexity quota fallback.
- Kimi free (B). 2M context window. When Gemini's 1M is not enough for a big competitor dump. Public content only, conversations stored in China.
- DeepSeek free (C). R1 reasoning fallback when Claude and ChatGPT both burn. Public content only, same data rule as Kimi.

Plus primary-data tools (no AI, real numbers): Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, Meta Ad Library, Similarweb free, Reddit/Quora/X search.

### Write (draft copy)

Five chat models work as drafters. ChatGPT and Gemini lead, DeepSeek and Kimi fill in when the leaders cap or you need extra context room.

- ChatGPT free (A). Copy drafter. GPT-5.3 for first 10 messages per 5-hour window, then silently drops to GPT-5.3-mini.
- Gemini 2.5 Flash free (A). Co-equal drafter. Same model that compresses research in the layer above.
- DeepSeek free (B). Public-content drafter (landing copy is public; OK to use). Conversations stored in China.
- Kimi free (B). 2M context drafter. Same public-content rule.

### Build (page, capture, image, attribution, launch reel)

Three recipes for the page build. Full guide at /docs/build-paths.

- Recipe Chat (default): chat model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi) writes the static HTML, save as `index.html`, drag the folder to netlify.com/drop with a free Netlify account so the URL persists. Best output, simplest, no token cliff.
- Recipe Builder: Bolt or Lovable. Chat-driven build environment with a live preview pane. Daily token cap on both. Co-equal, pick by feel.
- Recipe Agent: Claude Code or Codex in your terminal. The agent reads your context files and writes the build. Free with the India ChatGPT Go 12-month unlock for Codex (gives Codex sustained access free for a year for +91 SIM holders).

Other build and ship tools:

- Netlify free + Drop (S). Drag-and-drop hosting for the landing page folder. Sign up for the free account so URLs persist.
- Tally free (S). Waitlist forms. Unlimited submissions under 50K per month. Button-link from any Netlify or Bolt host.
- Tella free (A). Screen-and-camera recorder with branded UI. 25 min per clip on free tier. Use for async build updates and Demo Day pre-records. Pair with Loom (5-min clips).
- Gemini Nano Banana Standard free (A). Workhorse image. 100 per day.
- Gemini Nano Banana Pro free (A). Hero shot upgrade only. 2 per day.
- gpt-image-2 free in ChatGPT (A). 2 to 3 per day. Only OpenAI image model that renders dense text in Hindi, Bengali, Japanese, Korean, Chinese.
- Ideogram free (A). Hero image alt. 10 credits per day, roughly 40 images.
- Dub.co free (A). 25 short links per month. Track which post converted.
- CapCut free (A). Hand-assembled launch reels.
- OpusClip free (B). 60 minutes per month for auto-cut clips.

### India regional unlock (optional)

- ChatGPT Go free 12 months for +91 SIM holders. Verifies via UPI or card with a small refunded charge. Unlocks GPT-5.3 Instant, image gen, file uploads, extended memory, sustained Codex access. Auto-renews at ₹399/month after 12 months unless cancelled. Set a phone reminder for month 11.
- Razorpay (India only). Hosted checkout with UPI, cards, netbanking. Payment Pages for a production CTA link tied to your One Metric, or test mode for a demo "click to pay" flow without real charges. Stripe Payment Link if you're US/global.

### Avoid

- Webflow (learning curve), Wix, v0.dev (credits die in hours), Replit Agent (trial expires), Typeform free (10 responses max), Beehiiv and Substack on Day 1 (week-2 tools), Cursor and Windsurf (code-first, less context-aware than Claude Code and Codex), Notion sites (publishable but the muscle doesn't transfer).
- Voice and video AI that blocks commercial use on free tier: ElevenLabs, Pika, Luma, Runway, Suno. Launch reels are commercial content. Use CapCut + OpusClip + Nano Banana stills instead.

### Free-tier traps

- ChatGPT silently downgrades after 10 messages per 5-hour window. Watch the output quality.
- Canva AI is 50 LIFETIME image generations, Nano Banana Standard is 100 per day, Nano Banana Pro is 2 per day for hero shots only, Perplexity is 3 Pro Searches per day. Route accordingly.
- Bolt: 300K daily token cap is the binding constraint, not the 1M monthly umbrella. Evaporates on one complex build. Do all Bolt work in one focused 4-hour block on Day 1. After deploy, switch to manual edits in the `index.html` file.
- Lovable: 5 credits per day, 30 per month cap. Roughly 2 to 3 prompts per day. Plan prompts.
- Codex: free trial allowance burns quickly. Sustained access needs ChatGPT Plus or the India ChatGPT Go unlock.
- OpusClip free clips expire 3 days after generation. Download every Day 3 launch reel immediately or they vanish.
