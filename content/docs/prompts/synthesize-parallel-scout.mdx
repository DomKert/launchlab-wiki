## Why parallel beats single

One AI scout gives you one angle. Three give you the contradictions, the gaps, and the consensus. Contradictions are the most useful part because that's where weak claims expose themselves. Gemini's 1M context window collapses all three outputs into one brief so Claude doesn't have to.

## Why this works

You ran 3 scouts in parallel (Perplexity, Grok, Gemini) and now have 3 raw outputs. This prompt collapses them into one 500-word brief you upload to your Claude Project as `research-brief.md`. Forcing the synthesis to surface contradictions across the 3 sources is the whole point. Averaging the 3 outputs is what loses the signal. Disagreement is where weak claims expose themselves.

## How to feed Gemini the 3 outputs

Two equally good paths. Pick whichever feels faster.

- **Attach a single .md file.** Copy-paste from each scout tab into one `research-dump.md` on your machine, attach to the Gemini chat, run the prompt. Source labels are optional. Gemini reads structure and citation style well enough to keep the 3 sources distinct.
- **Paste inline.** Paste all 3 outputs directly into the chat under the prompt. Optionally label them with `--- PERPLEXITY ---` / `--- GROK ---` / `--- GEMINI ---` separators to make the contradictions step easier.

## Prompt

```txt
I ran the same niche scout query through 3 AI tools in parallel (Perplexity, Grok DeepSearch, Gemini). The 3 raw outputs are either pasted below or attached as a single research-dump.md. Source labels may or may not be visible inside the dump.

Synthesize the 3 outputs into a single brief I'll upload to my Claude Project as research-brief.md. Output exactly these 3 sections, nothing else:

1. COMPETITOR MAP. 5 to 8 entries, deduped across the dump. For each: name, one-line positioning, who they target, one weakness, URL.

2. CONTRADICTIONS. Where does the dump contain conflicting claims about facts, real players, or what counts as a gap? Quote the disagreement directly. Don't smooth it over. If everything is consistent, say "no contradictions found" and move on.

3. GAPS I COULD WIN. The 1 to 2 gaps in this market I could actually win as a first-time builder shipping in 3 days. For each: one sentence on the gap, evidence from at least 2 distinct passages of the dump that supports it.

Rules:
- Keep the entire brief under 500 words.
- No introduction, no closing, no sign-off. Plain markdown headings only.
- If a section of the dump is empty or missing, ignore it and proceed with the others.
- Treat distinct formatting or citation styles in the dump as a hint that the passages came from different scouts, even when source labels are missing.

[ATTACH research-dump.md OR PASTE 3 SCOUT OUTPUTS BELOW]
```

After Gemini outputs the brief, save the response as `research-brief.md` and upload it to your Claude Project knowledge (Files +). Every downstream prompt (the 4-box, landing-page spec, room scout) reads from this file.

## Where else to fan out

The same fan-out-and-compare pattern works on more than research.

- **Landing builder bake-off.** Same spec into 2 builders (Bolt and Framer, or Bolt and Lovable). Ship the one you like the look of, archive the other for ideas.
- **Hero image generation.** Same image brief into 2 generators (Nano Banana and Ideogram). Pick the one that lands the wordmark cleanest. See [Prompt 18 Hero image prompt](/docs/prompts/hero-image-prompt) for the multi-option flow.
- **Caption draft.** Same image and post intent into ChatGPT and Gemini side by side. Stitch the strongest sentences from each, run [Prompt 9 Strip AI smell](/docs/prompts/strip-ai-smell) on the merged version.

The synthesis is the unlock. One tool gives you one shape. Several give you the choice.
