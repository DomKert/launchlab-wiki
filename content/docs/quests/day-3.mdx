## The Raid Hour

Everyone publishes inside one 60-minute window. Each founder brings 8 to 10 genuine engagers from their own warm network. Engage with cohort peers and your own friends only. Platforms detect mass cross-engagement.

Post when your warm network is online. Around 25 of us, no fixed wave structure.

Reddit excluded. Post there 24 to 48h after the Raid Hour from your own karma-built account, 90/10 rule.

## Demo Day

Everyone who qualified presents their own work. 3-minute cap per founder. Around 25 expected (Cohort I pattern). At 3 minutes by 25 founders, the Demo Day session runs roughly 75 minutes.

You present your own thing. No relayed pitches, no captains. Stand up (or sit on camera), walk through what you built, what you tried, what moved your One Metric.
